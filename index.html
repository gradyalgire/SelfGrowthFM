PURPOSE

Why this exists

When life gets hard, the music you reach for can keep you stuck or help you move. It's easy to put on the songs that keep the wound open: the breakup song on repeat, the one that makes avoiding things feel normal.

SelfGrowth FM is the alternative: songs and media chosen because they lean toward responsibility, stability, and forward motion. It's built from personal experience, not trends or streaming numbers.

The goal isn't to feel better. It's to become better, and eventually, feeling better follows.

  • Picked by hand

    Every song and piece of media is reviewed manually. Nothing is added by an algorithm.

  • Every entry explains itself

    Each one has a description of why it's here, so you know what it's meant to help with before you press play.

  • Trigger warnings up front

    Heavy topics are listed on each card, so you can skip what you're not ready for.

  • Shaped by the people using it

    Anyone can suggest additions. Each suggestion is reviewed before it's added.

HOW IT WORKS

Using the site

Three pages, each with a different job. Here's what you can do on each one.

Playlist

The full song list, with a Spotify player at the top so you can listen while you browse.

  • Search by song number, title, artist, or description
  • Filter by genre to see songs in any genre you pick, or by category to see songs that match every category you pick
  • Click a cover to open the song in Spotify
  • Open the lyrics from any song card
Open the playlist

Media

Podcasts, books, videos, and movies, each on its own shelf. Use it as a resource, not a checklist.

  • Swipe or use the arrows to move through each shelf
  • Click a cover to open it at the source
  • Watch videos right on the page
Browse media

Recommendations

Suggest something that helped you, or flag a trigger warning that's missing.

  • Pick what you're suggesting: a song, media, a trigger, or something else
  • Explain specifically how it helped you. "I like this song" won't get it added.
  • Leave your email so you can get a follow-up
Song Media Trigger Other

Missing a trigger warning? Any song or media card without triggers has a Suggest Triggers link. It opens the recommendation form with that title already filled in.
